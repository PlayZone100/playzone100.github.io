The base formula

⚡ The "anything crisps" formula

You genuinely don't need a recipe most of the time. Learn this once:

🔥 The formula: any veg or leftover protein + a drizzle of oil + salt, pepper, whatever spice you like → 375–400°F for 8–15 minutes, shaking the basket halfway.

That single formula covers roasted vegetables, reheated fried food that actually stays crispy, crunchy chickpeas, re-crisped rotisserie chicken, and dozens of the recipes below.

Do this, not that

🚫 3 mistakes that ruin air fryer leftovers

🧺Overcrowding the basket
💧Skipping the oil drizzle
🔇Never shaking the basket

Overcrowding steams food instead of crisping it — cook in batches if needed. A light oil drizzle is what actually gets you the crunch, and shaking the basket halfway through ensures everything crisps evenly, not just the top layer.

Quick answers

❓ Frequently asked questions

What temperature should I use for most leftovers?

375–400°F works for almost everything. Higher heat crisps faster; lower heat is gentler on delicate leftovers like fish.

Do I need to preheat the air fryer?

Not strictly, but preheating 2–3 minutes gives a crispier result, especially for reheating fried food.

Can I air fry frozen vegetables straight from the freezer?

Yes — no need to thaw first. Frozen veg often comes out crispier than fresh roasted in the oven.

How much oil do I actually need?

A light drizzle or spray is enough — usually 1–2 teaspoons for a full basket. More than that just makes things soggy, not crispier.
