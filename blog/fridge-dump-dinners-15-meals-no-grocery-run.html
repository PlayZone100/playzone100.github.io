1. The 10-Minute Dump Pasta

Half box pasta + that jar of marinara + any cheese + frozen peas. Boil pasta, toss everything. Looks like you tried. Fridge Chef calls it "Pantry Marinara Medley".

2. Leftover Fried Rice That Tastes Like Takeout

Day-old rice + 1 egg + soy sauce + any wilted veg + that mystery meat. Hot pan, 6 minutes. Best hangover food ever.

3. Cheesy Tortilla Crush

Tortilla + shredded cheese + hot sauce + leftover chicken/beans. Fold, toast in pan. 4 mins, Instagram-worthy crunch.

4. The Everything Frittata

4 eggs + any vegetables that are about to die + cheese. Pour in pan, finish in oven 8 mins. Clears half your fridge in one go.

5. Loaded Toast 5 Ways

Toast + cream cheese/hummus + tomato + egg + herbs. This is what cafes charge $14 for. You have all of it already.

6. Fridge Forage Stir-Fry

Any protein + any two veg + soy + garlic + rice/noodles. The Fridge Chef AI literally invented this for me when I had broccoli, sausage and cold rice.
