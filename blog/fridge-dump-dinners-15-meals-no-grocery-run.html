Fridge Dump Dinners: 15 Meals That Look Expensive But Cost $0 Extra

Kitchen & Food Tech · 10 min read · No grocery run needed

Everyone is doing Fridge Dump Dinners on TikTok. You dump whatever is in your fridge onto the counter and turn it into something that looks like you planned it. I tested it for a week with Fridge Chef Pro - these 15 won.

1. The 10-Minute Dump Pasta

Half box pasta + jar marinara + any cheese + frozen peas. Boil, toss.

2. Leftover Fried Rice

Day-old rice + 1 egg + soy sauce + any wilted veg. Hot pan, 6 min.

3. Cheesy Tortilla Crush

Tortilla + shredded cheese + hot sauce + leftover chicken. Fold, toast.

4. The Everything Frittata

4 eggs + any dying vegetables + cheese. Pan + oven 8 mins.

5. Loaded Toast

Toast + cream cheese + tomato + egg + herbs. Cafe charges $14 for this.

6. Fridge Forage Stir-Fry

Any protein + any two veg + soy + garlic + rice.

7. Emergency Quesadilla

Tortilla + cheese + beans + salsa. 5 min.

8. Sad Veg Soup That Slaps

All soft veg + onion + broth cube + pasta. Blend half.

9. Breakfast Scramble Wrap

Eggs + potato leftovers + cheese + tortilla. Roll it.

10. Lazy Caprese-ish Bowl

Tomato + mozzarella + basil + olive oil + bread.

11. Peanut Noodles From Nothing

Noodles + peanut butter + soy + chili flakes.

12. Clean-Out-The-Cheese Mac

Any short pasta + all cheese ends + milk + mustard.

13. Leftover Chicken Salad

Chicken + yogurt + pickle + apple + bread.

14. Midnight Garlic Rice

Cold rice + lots garlic + butter + egg + soy.

15. Kitchen Sink Fried Egg

Fry 2 eggs + put whatever you have on top: salsa, cheese, beans.
