Pixel art looks like it came from a 90s game console: chunky squares, a small palette and bold shapes. It is also one of the most practical AI styles, because it stays readable at tiny sizes. That makes it perfect for Discord icons, gaming avatars and social profile pictures.

This guide covers which photos work best, a free workflow, what to do with the result and how to fix common problems. For other looks, see our Photo Trend Studio review and the AI photo trends overview.

1. Why pixel art works so well

Pixel art throws away fine detail on purpose. Skin texture, hair strands and small background objects all turn into a few colored squares. That is why small, simple subjects look great and busy photos turn into noise. If you can describe your photo in one sentence ("a smiling person in a red jacket"), pixel art will likely suit it.

2. The best photos for pixel art

A big subject

Crop closer. With few pixels to spend, a small face in a wide shot becomes a blob.

Strong contrast

Dark hair against a light wall, or a bright jacket against a plain background, keeps shapes separate after the blocks are applied.

A simple background

A plain wall, sky or solid color works best. Busy scenes turn into scattered colored squares.

Bold features

Glasses, hats, colorful clothing and clear hairstyles make a character recognizable in pixels.

Clear light

Even light without harsh shadows gives clean color areas. Heavy shadows become dark patches.
