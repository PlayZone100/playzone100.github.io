1. What it is

A couple portrait effect redraws two people together in one illustrated scene. The AI has to keep both identities separate and consistent, which is why couple photos need more preparation than single portraits.

The best results come from photos where the two people are close, similar in size and looking at the camera or each other.

3. Which photos work best

  • Both faces large: frame from the chest up.
  • Even light on both faces: avoid one person in shadow.
  • Similar head height: keep heads close and at similar angles.
  • No overlap on faces: hair or hands covering a face confuses the model.
  • Natural smiles: extreme expressions distort.
  • Original file: messaging copies lose detail that matters for print.

5. Prompt ideas

If your tool accepts text, keep prompts short and say what to keep from the photo:

Chibi couple version of this photo, big heads, small bodies, holding hands, same hair and glasses for both

Describe the look instead of naming a specific character, studio or living artist.

6. Printing and gift ideas

Printing: use the largest export available and avoid stretching a small image to a large poster. A square crop suits frames and phone cases.

  • Anniversary card or framed print
  • Wedding invitation or save-the-date illustration
  • Mug, phone case or phone wallpaper
  • Long-distance relationship surprise

Tip: add names or dates in an editor after generating, since AI often garbles text.

9. Ethics, copyright and privacy

  • Do not imitate specific characters, brands or living artists for commercial use.
  • Ask before stylizing other people, especially children.
  • Label AI-styled images so nobody mistakes them for unedited photos.
  • Ask your partner (and anyone else in the photo) before sharing the result publicly.
  • Never upload IDs, documents or private messages, and check the tool's terms before commercial use.
