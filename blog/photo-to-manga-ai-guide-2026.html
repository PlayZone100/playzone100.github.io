1. What it is

Manga is Japanese comic art, usually printed in black and white. A photo-to-manga effect converts your picture into ink outlines, hatching and screentone patterns, often with speed lines or dramatic backgrounds.

The AI simplifies shadows into a few tones and redraws the face with the exaggerated eyes and sharp features typical of the medium.

3. Which photos work best

  • Directional light: a window on one side creates the shadows manga uses.
  • High contrast: flat, gray, foggy photos give weak ink.
  • Large face: shoulders-up framing works best.
  • Visible eyes and hairline: manga is built around them.
  • Simple background: busy scenes turn into noisy pattern.
  • Sharp original: blur turns into messy lines.

6. Comic strips, couples and pets

Comic strips: make 3 or 4 photos with different expressions, then apply the same style to each and place them in a simple grid.

Couples: romance manga keeps both faces recognizable.

Pets: close-ups become charming mascot-style characters.

9. Ethics, copyright and privacy

  • Do not imitate specific characters, brands or living artists for commercial use.
  • Ask before stylizing other people, especially children.
  • Label AI-styled images so nobody mistakes them for unedited photos.
  • Do not copy a specific mangaka's signature style or a named character for commercial use.
  • Never upload IDs, documents or private messages, and check the tool's terms before commercial use.
