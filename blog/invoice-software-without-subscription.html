What a subscription still does better

We'd rather be straight about this than pretend one-time software has no downsides. A hosted subscription usually wins if you need:

  • Automatic email sending and payment reminders — Invoice Studio produces the PDF; you send it.
  • A shared workspace for a bookkeeper or team to log into from anywhere.
  • Automatic payment reconciliation tied to a bank feed.

If none of those apply to how you actually work — and for most solo freelancers and small studios, they don't — a one-time tool covers the job for a fraction of the cost.

Switching without losing your invoice history

If you're moving off a subscription, export your past invoices as PDFs before you cancel — most tools remove access to old records once the plan lapses.
