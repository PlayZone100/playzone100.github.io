The mental shortcut

⚡ The 20-minute dinner rule

Almost any dinner can be built from 4 categories:

🥩A protein
🍚A carb
🥦A vegetable
🧂A flavor boost
🎯 Pick one from each category, combine with heat, and you have dinner. Once this clicks, "what's for dinner" stops being a stressful question.

Quick answers

❓ Frequently asked questions

What's the fastest realistic weeknight dinner?

Egg fried rice using leftover rice — about 10 minutes, using ingredients most people already have.

How do I stop defaulting to takeout on busy nights?

Keep a small rotation of "no-think" recipes you can make on autopilot, and keep flexible staples like eggs, cheese, canned beans, and frozen veg stocked.

What pantry staples make the biggest difference?

Canned beans, canned tomatoes, rice, pasta, tortillas, and a few sauces can turn almost any fresh ingredient into a full meal in minutes.

Are frozen vegetables a good substitute for fresh?

Yes — frozen veg is picked and frozen at peak ripeness, often cooks faster than fresh, and never goes to waste before you get to it.
