1. Why painting styles tend to keep the likeness

An AI style tool reads what is in the photo, such as a face, fur, a jacket or a skyline, and redraws it with a new texture and palette. Cartoon styles change shapes heavily: bigger eyes, simpler outlines. Painting styles mostly change surface: brushstrokes, soft edges, paper grain. The structure of the face stays closer to the original, so people usually still recognize the subject. Results vary by photo, so always compare a few versions.

3. The best photos for painting styles

Soft, even light

Window light or open shade shows the AI the shape of the face without harsh shadows. Avoid strong backlight and flash, which flatten color.

A sharp subject

Painting styles add texture, so blur in the original becomes mush. Use the sharpest frame you have.

A big face or clear subject

Crop closer before uploading. If the subject fills a small part of the frame, the details are guessed.

A calm background

A plain wall, a blurred garden or a sky works best. Busy backgrounds can turn into odd shapes in oil paintings.

Natural color

Strong color casts from indoor bulbs carry into the painting. Shoot near a window or fix white balance first.

5. Which subjects work best

  • Pets. Watercolor and oil keep markings and eye color. See our pet photo guide.
  • Couples and families. Keep every face large and evenly lit. Our couple portrait guide has more tips.
  • Single portraits. Oil painting gives a classic, formal look that suits profile pictures and gifts.
  • Travel and landscapes. Watercolor turns holiday photos into postcard art.
  • Kids. Soft watercolor is gentle and keeps expressions natural.

6. Printing and framing your painting

Painting styles are print-friendly because the texture hides small imperfections. Start from the highest-resolution original and download the result at the best quality available. Ask your print shop for the recommended size at your file's resolution rather than stretching a small image. Matte or textured paper suits watercolor, and canvas suits oil.
