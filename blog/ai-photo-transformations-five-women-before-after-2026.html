One Photo, Five AI Transformations: Real Results and 50 Style Ideas (2026)

By the QuestMart team · Updated October 1, 2026 · About a 22 minute read

Quick answer: We took one ordinary travel selfie and turned it into five completely different worlds with Photo Trend Studio: an underwater scene, a galaxy portrait, a golden hour desert, a pastel anime street and a realistic Lisbon tram street. The face stayed recognizable every time. Below are all the results, what made each one work, a free workflow, and a list of 50 styles to try next, including 80s Portrait and Fairy Tale.

Most AI photo articles show one pretty result and move on. We wanted to answer a more useful question: what happens when you keep the person the same and change only the style? That is the fastest way to see what an AI photo tool can really do. A good tool should change the mood, the background, the colors and the art style, while the person in the picture still looks like herself.

So this article uses one source photo and five finished results, all made with our own tool, Photo Trend Studio. After the results we explain what we learned, how to repeat it for free, and then walk through 50 styles you can try on your own photos.

1. The original photo

The starting point is deliberately normal. It is a phone selfie taken outdoors, slightly grainy, with a busy background: a river, a stone tower and a stranger walking behind her. The face is large in the frame, the light is soft and even, and the expression is relaxed. That combination, a clear face and a messy background, is exactly the situation most people are in. They have a photo they like of themselves, in a place they do not want to keep.

We did not retouch the photo first. We uploaded it as it was and applied five different styles. Each result below is shown as the tool produced it, resized only for the web.

A note on consent: every person shown in an AI portrait should have agreed to it. Use your own photos, photos of friends and family who said yes, or licensed images. Never upload someone else's picture without permission, and label AI-made images when you share them.

2. How we judged the results

We looked at four things for each style. Likeness: does it still look like her? Style: does it truly look like the look we asked for? Detail: eyes, hair, hands and clothing. Use: would a real person post it, print it or use it as an avatar? Each result ends with a short verdict, including the weak spots, because honest notes are more useful than perfect-sounding ones.

3. Result 1: Underwater world

What changed: the river and tower were replaced by a bright blue ocean with rays of sunlight, floating particles and coral reefs. Her jacket became a light denim jacket with a white t-shirt, which suits the water palette.

What stayed: the dark wavy hair, the freckles, the red lipstick, the small gold hoop earring and the calm, slightly amused expression all carried over. The blue nail polish is still there too.

Verdict: likeness very good, style convincing, detail good. The soft light rays create a dreamy mood, and the dark hair stands out strongly against the blue. This one works well as a creative profile picture or a poster-style image. The only thing to check is the hand, which is partly cut off by the frame.

4. Result 2: Galaxy portrait

What changed: the background became a rich Milky Way sky with thousands of stars, and the whole picture was relit in deep blue and violet. Skin tones shifted to cool neon shades, with a pink glow on the cheek and shirt.

What stayed: the freckles, the shape of the eyes and brows, the lipstick and the earring. Even with a strong color change, she is clearly the same person.

Verdict: style excellent, likeness good, detail good. This is the most dramatic result of the five. It is perfect for music covers, gaming profiles and night-themed posts. Because the color is so strong, it does not suit formal use, and the skin looks very blue by design.

5. Result 3: Golden hour desert

What changed: a warm, glowing sunset replaced the grey river light. The scene became a sandy desert with a yellow tram on rails, mixing two ideas into one surreal travel image. A soft lens flare gives the whole picture a film look.

What stayed: the face and hair, with the light adding a golden edge. Her denim jacket turned a soft, washed blue-grey.

Verdict: style beautiful, likeness good, detail fair. The strong glow softens fine detail in the face, so it feels more like a dream than a documentary photo. It is a great choice for travel posts and aesthetic feeds. If you want more detail in the face, pick a style with less glare.

6. Result 4: Pastel anime street

What changed: the photo became a clean illustration in soft pink, lavender and sky blue. The eyes are larger and more expressive, the skin is smooth, and the street behind her is full of detail: flower boxes, shop awnings, balconies and a yellow tram.

What stayed: the hairstyle, the earring, the denim jacket, the white t-shirt and the gentle half-smile. In this style her hair shifted to a warm brown, which is a normal creative choice for anime palettes.

Verdict: style excellent, likeness good for a stylized look, detail very good. For avatars, recognizable beats exact. This is the best choice for a creator avatar, a profile picture or a book-cover style illustration.

7. Result 5: Realistic Lisbon street

What changed: this is the most realistic of the five. The background was rebuilt as a sunny city street with tiled facades, flower boxes, cobblestones and a yellow tram, while the film grain of the original disappeared and the colors became sharp and bright.

What stayed: everything that matters for likeness: freckles, dark hair, brown eyes, red lips, the gold hoop and the blue nails. It looks like a clean photo taken on a good camera in a different place.

Verdict: likeness excellent, style very convincing, detail very good. If your goal is a believable travel photo rather than an artwork, this is the type of result to aim for. Always label it as AI-made when you post it.

10. What we learned from the five results

A clear face survives almost any style

The source photo had a big, sharp face. That is why the likeness held up from realistic to anime to neon galaxy. The face is the anchor, and everything else can change around it.

The background is the easy part

A messy background was not a problem. In fact it is a reason to use a tool like this. A crowded street with strangers became a clean ocean, a night sky or a quiet tram street. If your best photo has a bad background, you do not have to throw it away.

Strong color means less skin detail

The galaxy and golden hour results are the most dramatic, and they also show the least natural skin. Glow and color grading are beautiful, but they hide freckles and texture. For a profile picture where people must recognize you, choose a calmer style.

Always compare two or three versions

The first result is often good, but the second or third can be better. Try one style twice, or the same photo in two styles, and keep the one that feels right.

Check eyes and hands

Eyes carry likeness and hands are the classic AI weak spot. Zoom in on both before you print or post.

12. 50 AI photo styles to try

Here are 50 styles, grouped by mood. Some are subtle and some change the whole scene. For each one we note what kind of photo works best. Use the names as a menu, and check the tool page for the styles available in your plan.

Retro and decades

  1. 80s Portrait. Soft focus, glowing neon edges, big hair energy and a studio backdrop in pink and teal. Best with a front-facing photo and clear light on the face.
  2. 90s Disposable Camera. Flash glare, slight grain and a casual party feel. Great for group-of-friends nostalgia and relaxed selfies.
  3. 70s Film. Warm orange and brown tones with soft grain, like an old family slide. Works well on outdoor photos.
  4. Vintage Polaroid. A faded instant-photo look with a pale border and slightly washed colors. Ideal for travel memories.
  5. Y2K Glam. Glossy skin, silver accents and sparkle, inspired by early 2000s magazines. Best with a confident pose.
  6. Retro Magazine Cover. Bold framing and a vintage print texture. Pick a head-and-shoulders photo.
  7. Noir Black and White. Deep shadows and strong contrast, like a classic detective film. Needs a photo with clear side light.
  8. Old Hollywood Glamour. Soft silvery light, elegant waves and a studio-portrait feel. Suits calm, straight-on expressions.

Art and illustration

  1. Pencil Sketch. Fine lines and delicate shading on white paper. Side light gives the best shadows.
  2. Charcoal. Heavy smudged shadows and rough texture. Beautiful on older faces and for family gifts.
  3. Watercolor. Soft washes of color that bleed together. Works best on bright photos with open sky or water.
  4. Oil Painting. Visible brush strokes and rich skin tones. A plain background and straight-on pose are safest.
  5. Pastel Drawing. Chalky, gentle colors with a handmade look. Good for portraits of children and soft, warm photos.
  6. Pop Art. Flat bold colors and dotted print effects. Use a high-contrast photo with a clear outline.
  7. Impressionist. Dabs of color and shimmering light, like a garden painting. Great for outdoor scenes.
  8. Ink Illustration. Clean black lines with small areas of color. Works well with strong profiles.
  9. Comic Book. Thick outlines, halftone shading and dramatic color. Choose an expressive pose.
  10. Stained Glass. Bright colored segments separated by dark lines. Best with simple shapes and a bold background.

Anime and cartoon

  1. Anime. Large eyes, clean lines and bright colors, as in our pastel street example above.
  2. Soft Pastel Anime. A gentler version with pink and lavender light, good for dreamy avatars.
  3. Chibi. A cute, small-bodied character with a big head. Best with a clear face and simple outfit.
  4. 3D Cartoon. Smooth, rounded features like an animated movie character. Works well with friendly smiles.
  5. Manga Panel. Black and white screentone shading and dramatic lines. Pick photos with strong expression.
  6. Storybook Illustration. Warm, hand-drawn scenes like a children's book page. Great for gifts.
  7. Sticker Pack. A simplified, outlined character ready to use as a chat sticker. Use a clear, cheerful face.

Fantasy and fairy tale

  1. Fairy Tale. Glowing forests, floating lights, flowing fabrics and a storybook atmosphere. Use a soft, well-lit portrait and expect a warm, magical mood with gentle color.
  2. Elf Portrait. Elegant features, delicate jewelry and misty woodland. Needs a clear view of the ears and hair.
  3. Mermaid World. Shimmering water, pearls and coral, close to our underwater result. Best with long hair.
  4. Enchanted Forest. Moss, mushrooms and rays of light through trees. Works with almost any portrait.
  5. Royal Portrait. A crown, rich fabrics and a palace background. Use a straight-on, confident pose.
  6. Dragon Rider. Dramatic skies and a fantasy creature behind you. Choose an action pose with clear lighting.
  7. Snow Queen. Icy blues, frosted crowns and falling snow. Best on cool-toned photos.

Sci-fi and futuristic

  1. Galaxy. Stars, nebulas and cosmic light, as in our result above. Good for dark hair and bold looks.
  2. Cyberpunk Neon. Rainy streets, pink and cyan signs and a high-tech mood. Works well with night photos.

Travel and scenery

Professional and seasonal

  3. Lookbook. Natural street-style framing for outfits and personal brands.

13. Tips for getting better results in any style

Start with light. Window light or late afternoon light gives the AI clear shapes to work with. Crop before you upload. A head-and-shoulders crop improves likeness in nearly every style. Use a simple pose. A relaxed, straight-on or slightly angled pose is the safest. Take selfies from farther away. Hold the phone at arm's length and zoom in, or ask a friend. This avoids wide-angle distortion. Keep the file sharp. A blurry original gives a blurry result in every style.

Do not choose a style only to look younger or different. Choose the one that fits the person and the goal. A style that matches the mood of the photo will always look more natural than one that fights it.

14. Printing and sharing

Start from your highest-resolution original and download the result at the best quality available. Ask your print shop for the recommended size at your file's resolution. Matte or lightly textured paper suits pencil and watercolor, heavier paper suits charcoal, and canvas suits oil. When posting, name the style in the caption and say that the image is AI-made. People enjoy before and after posts, and honest labels build trust.
