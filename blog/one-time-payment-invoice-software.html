What's deliberately not included

Keeping the price low and the payment one-time means Invoice Studio doesn't include things that require ongoing server infrastructure to run: automatic email sending, payment reminders, or a cloud sync between devices. Those cost a hosted vendor real, recurring money to operate, which is part of why they're billed monthly elsewhere.
