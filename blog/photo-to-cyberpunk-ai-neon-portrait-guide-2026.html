Cyberpunk is the look of rainy neon cities: deep blue and purple shadows, hot pink and cyan glow, glossy skin and futuristic mood. It is one of the boldest AI styles, and it turns an ordinary selfie into a movie poster or a game avatar.

This guide explains what makes the style work, which photos suit it, a free workflow, ideas for using the result and fixes for common problems. For a bigger picture of what is popular, see the AI photo trends overview.

1. What makes the cyberpunk look

Three ingredients define the style. First, color contrast: cool blue and purple in the shadows against warm pink and cyan highlights. Second, glow: bright edges, reflections and light on skin. Third, mood: dark, urban, a little futuristic. The AI adds these on top of your photo, so a photo that already has contrast and a darker tone gives a stronger result.

2. The best photos for cyberpunk

A clear face with side light

Light from one side gives the face shape, and the style turns it into a colored rim of light. Flat front flash looks dull.

Strong contrast

Dark hair, dark clothes and a mid-tone or dark background make neon colors pop.

A simple or dark background

A plain wall, dusk sky or a street at night works best. Bright, busy backgrounds can turn into a confusing color mix.

Night streets and city scenes

Wet roads, shop signs and car lights already look cyberpunk and become cinematic.

Visible eyes

Eyes carry the likeness. Avoid heavy shadows across them or sunglasses.
