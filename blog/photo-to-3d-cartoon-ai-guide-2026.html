1. What it is

A 3D cartoon effect redraws you as a computer-rendered character. The AI keeps your facial structure, hair and expression, then adds soft shading, rounded forms and stylized proportions.

Compared with 2D anime, 3D styles keep more depth and lighting, so photo lighting matters even more.

3. Which photos work best

  • Soft front light: shows the model your facial structure.
  • Shoulders-up framing: a big face gives more information.
  • Neutral expression or gentle smile: big grins distort.
  • Visible hair and glasses: they carry identity.
  • Plain background: gives a clean avatar edge.
  • Sharp original: 3D shading amplifies blur.

5. Prompt ideas

If your tool accepts text, keep prompts short and say what to keep from the photo:

Turn this person into a 3D cartoon character, glossy animated-movie look, same face, hair and glasses, soft cinematic lighting, plain background
Clay-style 3D character of this person, handmade texture, warm colors, friendly smile
3D vinyl toy avatar of this person, big head, shiny plastic, studio light, square crop

Describe the look instead of naming a specific character, studio or living artist.

9. Ethics, copyright and privacy

  • Do not imitate specific characters, brands or living artists for commercial use.
  • Ask before stylizing other people, especially children.
  • Label AI-styled images so nobody mistakes them for unedited photos.
  • Do not name a specific animation studio or character in prompts if you plan commercial use.
  • Never upload IDs, documents or private messages, and check the tool's terms before commercial use.
