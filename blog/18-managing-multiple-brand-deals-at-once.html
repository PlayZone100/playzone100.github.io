Managing Five Brand Conversations Without Dropping One

At some point, "I'll just remember where things stand" stops working — you've got one brand waiting on a contract, another waiting on your reply, and a third you genuinely forgot replied to you a week ago.

A pipeline needs stages, not just a yes/no. Contacted, Negotiating, Confirmed, Content Due, Paid — a deal moves through real stages, and a system that only tracks "done" or "not done" hides where things are actually stuck.

Set a follow-up date the moment you send anything. The deals that die aren't the ones brands reject — they're the ones that go quiet because nobody followed up. A simple rule: every outbound message gets a follow-up date attached before you move on to the next task.

Track payment status separately from content status. "Posted" and "Paid" are not the same milestone, and treating them as one is how creators end up chasing invoices for content that went live weeks ago. Some brands pay on posting, others net-30 or net-60 — know which for each deal.

Keep brand-specific notes, not just the deal terms. Who you talked to, their tone, what they cared about in the brief — this context is what makes your second deal with the same brand faster and smoother than the first, and it's exactly what gets lost if it only lives in your email history.

Review the whole pipeline weekly, not deal-by-deal as they come to mind. A ten-minute weekly scan of every active deal catches the ones that have gone quiet before they've been quiet for a month.
