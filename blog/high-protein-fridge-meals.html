Quick answers

❓ Frequently asked questions

What's the easiest way to hit a high protein target without meal prepping all day?

Stock 3–4 zero-effort staples (eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, canned tuna or beans) so the fastest option in your fridge is automatically a high-protein one.

Is cottage cheese actually a good protein source?

Yes — it's one of the highest-protein-per-calorie dairy options available, and needs zero preparation.

Do I need protein powder to hit my goals?

Not necessarily. Whole-food sources like eggs, chicken, tofu, and Greek yogurt can cover most people's needs without any supplements, though a shake can help fill gaps on busy days.

How much protein should I aim for per meal?

General guidance from nutrition research suggests roughly 20–40g per meal supports muscle maintenance and growth well, though individual needs vary — a registered dietitian can help tailor this to you.
