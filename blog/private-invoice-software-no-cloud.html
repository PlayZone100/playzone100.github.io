What's actually inside an invoice

An invoice looks like a simple document, but it's a small dossier: who you work for, what they pay, how often, and increasingly, their crypto wallet address if you invoice in USDT or Bitcoin. Sign up for a hosted invoicing tool and that data — for every client you've ever billed — sits in a database you don't control, protected by someone else's security practices, subject to someone else's breach history.

That's not a reason to panic about every SaaS tool. It's a reason some freelancers and small agencies specifically look for private invoice software that never uploads anything in the first place.

How Invoice Studio keeps data local

Invoice Studio is a single HTML file that runs entirely in your browser. There is no server, no API call, no account. Concretely:

  • Client names, amounts and notes are stored in your browser's local storage, on your device, full stop
  • No analytics, no tracking pixel, no "usage data" sent anywhere
  • Works with your internet connection off, which is also a quick way to confirm nothing is being uploaded

Nobody — including the makers of Invoice Studio — can see your client list, your rates, or your invoice history. It never reaches us.

The trade-off: backups are on you

Keeping everything local means you're responsible for backing it up. There's no cloud copy to fall back on if you clear your browser data or switch computers. Invoice Studio's Backup section exports everything to one JSON file with one click, and restoring is just as fast. Get in the habit of exporting it monthly, or right before any browser cleanup — it takes about ten seconds and protects your entire invoice history.

Who this matters most for

Local-only invoicing is a natural fit for:

  • Consultants under NDA, where client identities themselves are confidential
  • Crypto freelancers who'd rather not have wallet addresses sitting in a third-party database
  • Agencies billing high-value contracts, where the invoice amounts are commercially sensitive
  • Anyone billing across borders who prefers not to have client data subject to a foreign vendor's jurisdiction

If that's the kind of work you do, Invoice Studio is built around exactly that requirement.

Frequently asked questions

Does Invoice Studio send any data to a server?

No. It makes no network calls at all. You can disconnect your internet entirely and it works exactly the same.

Where is my data actually stored?

In your browser's local storage on the device you're using — nowhere else, unless you export a backup file yourself.

What if I lose my computer or clear my browser?

Without a recent backup, that data is gone, the same way a local spreadsheet would be. Export a backup regularly using the built-in Backup feature.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes — the licence covers commercial use on as many of your own devices as you like. Use the JSON backup export/import to move your data between them.
