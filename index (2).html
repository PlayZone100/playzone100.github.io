Fridge Chef: Recipes From What You Have

Most people do not need more recipes; they need a way to cook with the food already in the house. These guides start from that idea: what to make from leftovers, how to cut food waste, how to meal prep once for a week and how to feed picky eaters or a tight budget. Fridge Chef, our photo-to-recipe tool, turns a picture of your fridge into three dinner ideas, and the guides show the thinking behind it.
