Good to know before you buy

A few honest details, so the tool fits how you work.

It's one HTML file, not a hosted app
You get a single file to open in your browser or upload to your own site. There's no login and no server on our side.
Your data lives in your browser
Your calendar, income, deals, ideas and logo are saved in this browser only. Use Export all data at the top of the page regularly, and before you clear history or switch devices. Import backup restores it.
Video export depends on your browser
Export uses your browser's built-in recorder. It works in current Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Safari and iPhone may not support it. Export runs in real time, so a 30-second clip takes about 30 seconds.
Two AI tools download a model once
Background removal and auto-captions fetch a model of about 40 MB the first time you use them, so you need internet for that moment. After that they work offline. Some school and office networks block the download.
AI writing is optional and uses your key
The Write with AI button sends your text to Anthropic with your own API key, billed to your own Anthropic account. The built-in draft generator works without it.
Estimates are starting points
The rate estimator is not a market benchmark, and the tax set-aside is a planning number, not tax advice.

Questions

How do I pay, and how do I get the file?

Choose Buy, pick Bitcoin, Ethereum, or USDT (ERC-20 or TRC-20), and the checkout shows the amount, a wallet address and a QR code. When the payment confirms, your download starts automatically. There is no account to create.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Unzip the download and open creator-command-center.html in your browser, or upload it to your own site and open it from there.

Does it need an internet connection?

No, it works offline. There are two exceptions: the optional Write with AI button, and the first use of background removal or auto-captions, which download a model of about 40 MB that your browser then keeps.

Where is my data stored, and can I move it?

In your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing you type or upload is sent to a server. Use Export all data (.json) to make a backup and Import backup to restore it on this device or a new one.

Do I need an AI account to use it?

No. The draft generator works on its own. If you want AI-written drafts, paste your own Claude API key into the AI mode field. Usage is billed to your Anthropic account, and the key is stored only in your browser.

Which browsers work?

It's built for current Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Video export relies on the browser's recorder, which Safari and iPhone may not support.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes, on your own devices. The license covers one person running their own creator business. Please don't resell, redistribute or re-publish the file itself.

How accurate is the rate estimator?

It's a starting point, not a market benchmark. It uses your views, engagement, deliverables and a CPM you can adjust to your niche and audience.
