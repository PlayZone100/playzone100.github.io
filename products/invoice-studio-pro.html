Built for people who bill across borders

Most invoice tools assume one country, one currency and one tax rule. Freelance work has not looked like that for a decade.

150+ currencies, crypto included

Each currency formats the way its own market expects. Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, USDC, Solana and Monero are handled at proper decimal precision instead of being squeezed into two places.

Wallet addresses on the invoice

List as many wallets as you accept alongside your bank details. They print as selectable text, so a client can copy an address straight out of the PDF without retyping it.

Tax that matches your country

No tax, a single rate, or a different rate on every line. Prices can be tax-exclusive or tax-inclusive, which is the difference between a correct and an incorrect VAT invoice.

Seven document languages

Send a German client a Rechnung and a Brazilian client a Fatura from the same file. Only the printed labels change — your own wording stays exactly as you typed it.

Right-to-left, properly

Choose Arabic or Persian and the whole document flips: columns, totals, the paid stamp, all of it. Not a mirrored afterthought.

Four print templates

Classic sets the document in a serif for a formal, printed feel. Modern is clean and contemporary. Compact fits long itemised jobs on one page. Typed is monospaced and plain. Eight accent colours and your own logo.

Clients remembered

Save a client once and fill their details in with one click next month. Duplicate last month's invoice and change two numbers — retainer billing takes under a minute.

Part payments and deposits

Record a deposit already received and the document shows the balance due underneath the total, the way a client expects to read it.

Your books, exportable

A single JSON file backs up everything for a new computer. A CSV export lists every document with its total, ready for a spreadsheet or your accountant at year end.

Questions people ask first

Does it really work with no internet?
Yes. Disconnect and try it — nothing changes. There is no server, no API call and no tracking of any kind. That is also why nobody, including us, can see your clients or your figures.
Where are my invoices stored?
In your browser's local storage on that computer. Use the Backup export to move everything to another machine, or to keep a copy before clearing your browser data. It takes one click and produces one file.
Can I put my own logo and branding on it?
Yes. Upload a logo, pick an accent colour, choose a template. If you know a little CSS you can change anything else, and the licence allows you to rebrand it for your own business.
Does it make a real PDF?
It prints through your browser to a proper A4 PDF, which is what every invoicing tool does underneath. The README lists the three print settings that give the cleanest result.
Can I use it for client work?
Yes — commercial use is included, on as many of your own devices as you like. The one thing the licence does not allow is reselling or redistributing the file itself.
Will it keep working?
It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no dependencies, so it has nothing to break and nothing to update. The copy you download today will open in a browser years from now exactly as it does now.
How do I receive it after paying?
Pay in BTC, ETH or USDT. As soon as the payment confirms on-chain, the download unlocks automatically — no email back and forth and no waiting on anyone to approve it.
