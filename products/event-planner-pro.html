FAQ

Can I use Event Planner Pro without an internet connection?

Yes, Event Planner Pro is a standalone application that requires no internet connection.

Is my data safe?

Your data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage and is not shared or transmitted anywhere.

Can I switch between light and dark themes?

Absolutely, you can easily toggle between light and dark themes to suit your preferences.

Is there a demo mode available?

Yes, you can try out Event Planner Pro in demo mode by setting window.QM_DEMO to true in your browser's console.

How do I clear all my data?

You can clear all your data by clicking on the 'Clear All' button in the settings menu.
