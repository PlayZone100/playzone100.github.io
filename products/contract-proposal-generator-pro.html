Questions people ask first

Is a drawn signature in this tool legally binding?
In most jurisdictions, an electronic signature is legally valid as long as both parties intend to be bound by it — the same principle major e-signature platforms rely on. This tool is not a substitute for legal advice on your specific situation, but the drawn signature, timestamp and integrity code together give you meaningfully stronger evidence of agreement than a typed name alone.
Does it really work with no internet?
Yes. Disconnect and try it — nothing changes. There is no server, no API call and no tracking. That is also why nobody, including us, can see your contracts or your clients.
Where are my contracts stored?
In your browser's local storage on that computer. Use the Backup export to move everything to another machine, or to keep a copy before clearing your browser data.
Can both parties sign on the same device?
Yes — Party A signs first, then hands the device to Party B (or shares the screen on a call) to sign in their own box. Each signature gets its own timestamp regardless of order.
Can I use my own contract wording instead of the templates?
Yes. Every clause — confidentiality, intellectual property, liability, termination — is a plain text box you can rewrite completely. The templates are a starting point, not a restriction.
Will it keep working?
It's plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no dependencies, so it has nothing to break and nothing to update. The copy you download today will open in a browser years from now exactly as it does now.
