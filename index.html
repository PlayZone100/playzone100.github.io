How the Freelance Hub fee works

When a project is completed and approved, the freelancer receives 80% of the agreed budget and QuestMart keeps a 20% platform fee to run and support the marketplace. This fee is always displayed on the project page before you accept or post anything — there are no other charges. Questions about a payment? support@questmart.online any time.
