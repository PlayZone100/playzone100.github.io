Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

QuestMart ("we," "us") operates questmart.online, a marketplace for digital products and a freelance projects hub ("Freelance Hub"). This page explains what information we collect, why, and how you can reach us about it.

Information we collect

  • Digital product purchases: your email address and payment details are processed by our payment provider to deliver your product and send your receipt. We do not store full card numbers ourselves.
  • Freelance Hub accounts: if you create an account to post or accept projects, we store your name, email, and the project details you submit (titles, descriptions, budgets, messages).
  • Basic site analytics: standard, aggregated technical data (like page views) that helps us understand which parts of the site are useful.

How we use your information

  • To deliver the product or service you purchased or requested.
  • To respond to support requests sent to support@questmart.online.
  • To operate Freelance Hub — matching posted projects with freelancers, and processing approvals and payouts.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-party services

We rely on a small number of third-party services to run QuestMart — for example, a payment processor to handle purchases, and hosting/infrastructure providers to keep the site running. These providers only receive the information needed to perform their function, and each has its own privacy practices.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing us. If you have a Freelance Hub account, you can also request full account deletion.

Contact

Questions about this policy?

Email support@questmart.online — a real person on our five-person team will respond, usually within 1–2 business days.
