Frequently asked questions

How does the AI plant diagnosis work?

Upload a clear photo of the affected leaves or plant, add any notes about watering or light, and the AI analyzes visual symptoms — spots, discoloration, wilting, pests — to suggest likely causes and next steps.

Is Plant Doctor Pro free to try?

Yes — every visitor gets one free photo diagnosis and a few free chat messages before upgrading is required.

What plants does it work with?

Houseplants, garden vegetables, flowers, succulents and trees — ask or upload a photo of any plant you're growing.

Can it replace a professional horticulturist?

It's a fast first opinion, not a replacement. For valuable or rare plants, or a problem that isn't improving, check with a local nursery or horticulturist.
